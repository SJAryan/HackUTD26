Dispatch logic and the canonical demo
-------------------------------------

Store UTC timestamps. For new evidence, anchor bounds to the observed-at time, normally the
reply time. Ask for clarification if the message describes an older reading ambiguously. Store
absolute deadlines once. A duplicate event cannot restart the clock.

deadline_lo = observed_at + reported_lower_minutes
deadline_hi = observed_at + reported_upper_minutes      (if bounded)
at_asset    = max(now, driver_free_at) + travel(driver_free_location, asset_location)
depart      = max(at_asset, asset_free_at) + pickup_minutes
ready_at    = depart + travel(asset_location, home) + setup_minutes
margin_lo   = deadline_lo - ready_at
margin_hi   = deadline_hi - ready_at                    (if bounded)

Evaluate compatible driver, resource and route combinations. Show the selected option and the
best available alternative separately. Under 30 minutes has a zero lower bound. Two or more
hours has no finite upper bound. Use an appointment or pickup target only when confirmed. For a
job already in progress, use its last progress and ETA instead of restarting the whole trip on
every refresh.

Silent
  Rule — The current check-in round is overdue without a verified response. Preserve last
  evidence, current risks, callback owner and due time.

Unknown
  Rule — A reply or first check-in lacks an actionable time window. Keep a clarification task
  and owner. Five minutes is a configurable demo follow-up interval.

Act now
  Rule — Known evidence with conservative margin under 15 minutes, or an unresolved case needing
  a resource with none eligible. Surface uncovered work even when another lane tracks contact.

Watch
  Rule — Known evidence with best conservative margin ≥15 minutes.

Contact, deadline evidence and assistance are independent fields. Compute coverage from distinct
households with a verified resident or authorized proxy reply in the selected window divided by
all enrolled households in the incident. Show open needs and overdue callbacks separately. An
operator can override a lane with an audited reason. A scheduled reducer refreshes policy
transitions and checks due follow-ups. Subscribe clients to changes and use display-side ticks
between sweeps.

Worked example. At 02:00 the household reports 30–60 minutes of backup, giving a reported window
of 02:30–03:00. Route R4 originally allows setup at 02:13, so its margin is +17…+47. A confirmed
20-minute detour moves R4 to 02:33, or −3…+27. A compatible alternate route ready at 02:24
offers +6…+36. On the route comparison, show R4 changing from +17 to −3. On the household card,
the best option changes from +17 to +6 and the case moves from Watch to Act now. If every route
misses the lower estimate, show the shortfall and a manual escalation. Seed times relative to
the live report for judging and use a fixed clock for replay.
